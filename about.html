PECUNIA

A personal finance tracker that keeps your data in your own Google Drive.

PECUNIA helps you keep track of your own money in one place: what you spend, what you hold in your accounts, and what you have lent or owe. It is built for individuals who want a simple record of their finances without handing their data to a third party.

Why PECUNIA asks to connect to Google

Signing in with Google is optional. If you connect, PECUNIA asks for a single permission, drive.file, so it can save your data as one file named MidnightWealthData.json in your own Google Drive and load it back on your other devices. With this permission PECUNIA can only access the file it created. It cannot see, list or open any other file in your Drive.

You can also use PECUNIA without connecting Google. In that case your data stays only in your browser on your device.

Your data stays yours
