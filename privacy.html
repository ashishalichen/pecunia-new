Privacy Policy

PECUNIA · Last updated: November 13, 2026 · About PECUNIA

PECUNIA (“the app”, “we”) is a personal finance tracker available at pecunia-personal-finance.vercel.app. It helps you record your spending, accounts, and money you have lent or owe. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • Your financial entries are stored in your browser on your device and, if you connect Google, in a file in your own Google Drive.
  • The only Google permission we request is drive.file.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

2. Information the app handles

Data you enter. Purchases, categories, account balances, savings, amounts lent or owed, and similar entries. This is stored in your browser’s local storage on your device.

Google user data (only if you connect Google). When you choose “Continue with Google”, the app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This lets the app create and update one file in your Google Drive named MidnightWealthData.json, which contains the same data you entered in the app. With this scope the app cannot view, list, read, or modify any other file in your Drive. The app does not request your contacts, calendar, email, or other Google data.

Sign-in credentials. To keep your device connected, the app’s sync service on our hosting provider (Vercel) handles the Google sign-in tokens needed to reach your PECUNIA file. These tokens are used only for that purpose.

Technical data. Like any website, the hosting provider may process standard request information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site. We do not use this for tracking or advertising.

3. How we use the information

We use the information only to provide the app’s features: showing your finances, saving a backup of your data to your Drive, and restoring it on your devices. We do not use your data for any other purpose.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your data or transfer it to third parties. Your data is not shared with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information only if required by law. Our hosting provider processes requests on our behalf to run the site.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

PECUNIA’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, we use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above, we do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide those features or comply with law, we do not use it for advertising, and no humans read it unless you give permission, it is needed for security or to comply with law.

6. Storage, security and retention

Your data is transmitted over HTTPS. The local copy stays in your browser until you clear it. The Drive file stays in your Drive until you delete it. We do not keep a separate copy of your financial entries on our servers.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Disconnect Google: remove PECUNIA’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the Drive file: delete MidnightWealthData.json from your Google Drive.
  • Delete the local copy: clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

8. Children

PECUNIA is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date above shows the latest version.
